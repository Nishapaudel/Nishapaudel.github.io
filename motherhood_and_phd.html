Motherhood and PhD

By Nisha Paudel · 17 November 2026

I became a mother during my PhD. Being a mother has its pros and cons. Becoming a mother at any stage of life is a challenge, but it’s a beautiful challenge.

My PhD is my baby too. Research is my passion, and it’s what I’d love to do for the rest of my life. That’s the professional part of my life. On the personal side, I love babies. I’d been thinking about becoming a mother for a couple of years, especially when I was working in a nursing home caring for older people.

My supervisor is an amazing person. Every time I talk to her, she has something positive to say. I feel so empowered and motivated after meeting with her. Also, my team mates, HDR staffs, Deakin University has supported me. I had five months paid maternity leave which is amazing.

My daughter is magical. Every time I see her, all my stress vanishes. Cuddling and playing with her leaves me feeling refreshed. Life has become so busy that I don’t have time to stress about problems that might come up in the future.
